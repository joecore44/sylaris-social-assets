# Sylaris social brand rules

Taken from the Sylaris 30-Day Social Launch Brief (Oct 2026). Every new post and image follows these.

## Positioning

- Primary line: *The operating system for modern consumer healthcare brands.*
- Core message: *You build the healthcare brand. We built the infrastructure behind it.*
- Primary call to action: Book a Launch Call at sylarisos.com/launch.
- Audience: entrepreneurs, creators and operators with an audience; clinic and med-spa owners; wellness, fitness and supplement brands adding telehealth; existing telehealth operators tired of a stitched-together stack.
- Sylaris is not an EMR. The founder is "Joe" (or "Joe Michael"), never any other surname.

## Visual system

- Sora headlines (tight, large), Inter body, teal accent #16D6C8, blue glow #4AA8FF.
- Two backgrounds: dark (near-black with teal and blue glows and a faint grid) and light (white with soft aurora glows).
- Pill label top left with a teal dot. Key phrase of the headline in teal (dark) or gray (light).
- SYLARIS wordmark bottom left, `sylarisos.com/launch` bottom right (or the carousel counter).
- No stock photos, no new visual identity, no emoji in images.
- Sizes: feed 1080×1350, X 1600×900, story 1080×1920 (keep the top 210 px and bottom 200 px clear).

## Captions

- One idea per post. Short, specific sentences. Hooks that state a point ("Your patients see your brand. Not ours.").
- Facebook: ends with `Link: sylarisos.com/launch` (UTM link in the post's link field).
- Instagram: ends with `Link in bio.` then a blank line and `#telehealth #digitalhealth #healthtech #founders #telehealthbusiness`.
- X: no link in the post body; the link goes in the first reply. Under 280 characters unless it's a thread.
- UTM: `?utm_source=<platform>&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=<campaign>&utm_content=<post id>`.

## Claims: say it like this, never like that

| Claim | Say it like this | Never |
| --- | --- | --- |
| AI Recovery | "Current testing has shown nearly a 40% improvement in recovered intake completions." | "40% more patients", "guaranteed" |
| LegitScript | "Expedited review can take as little as 7–14 days; LegitScript decides." | "Guaranteed approval", a fixed date |
| Launch time | "Typically live in 4–5 weeks" | "Live in 30 days, guaranteed" |
| Providers | "Licensed providers in all 50 states + Puerto Rico" plus the availability note | Every treatment available everywhere |
| Economics | Always "illustrative, not a projection" plus the exclusions | "You'll make $15K/month" |
| Founder results | "Companies Joe helped build and scale" + "past results don't predict yours" | Implying Joe personally runs your brand |
| AP3X Health | "$0 → $350K MRR in 60 days, built on Sylaris OS" + "individual results vary" | Acquisition talk or revenue projections |
| Compliance | "HIPAA-compliant" | SOC 2 claims until confirmed |
| Market | "$41.7B (2025) → $88.2B (2030), 16.1% CAGR, Grand View Research" | Uncited stats |

- Never invent statistics. Label anything unverified [VERIFY] and don't publish it.
- Dashboard screenshots are sample data; say so.
- Prescription drug names (semaglutide, TRT, sermorelin, GLP-1) only in organic Instagram, Facebook and X posts. Never on TikTok, never boosted.
